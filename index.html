The Machine-to-Machine Payment Protocol

Autonomous AI agents cannot hold corporate credit cards, pass KYC, or commit to $50/month SaaS subscriptions. Relay revives HTTP status 402, allowing agents to pay fractions of a cent ($0.04 - $0.12 USDC) per request directly from their wallet.

Open Marketplace Architecture: Beyond the 12 initial genesis feeds, Relay serves as a permissionless capability monetization network where any third-party data provider, proprietary AI model, or compute node can publish APIs and earn instant USDC micropayments via x402.

Designed for the Vibestarter raise, the $RELAY token has an indispensable Day-1 operational role within the gateway economics:

  • 50% Protocol Fee Discount: Queries settled in $RELAY receive an automatic 50% fee discount against standard USDC pricing, driving organic buy demand from agent operators.
  • Agent Quota Staking: AI agent swarms and enterprises stake $RELAY to unlock permanent, daily gasless query quotas without micropayment overhead.
  • Marketplace Provider Bonding: Third-party capability providers stake $RELAY as quality collateral against SLA violations, data corruption, or provider downtime.
  • 20% Programmatic Buyback & Burn: 20% of all gateway USDC settlement fees are programmatically routed to buy back and burn $RELAY on Base decentralized exchanges (Uniswap / Aerodrome).
